Let’s talk on WhatsApp ↗
What does New, Used and Refurbished mean?+

New means unused. Used means previously owned. Refurbished describes a previously owned device inspected or restored where applicable. Read the condition information for the specific device.

Does Boxed mean the phone is New?+

No. Boxed means the device is supplied with its box. Its actual condition is listed separately.

How do I order?+

Choose a device and tap its WhatsApp enquiry button. Confirm the current stock, price and condition, then agree payment and delivery or collection directly with us.

How do repairs work?+

Choose a repair service and send your brand, model and a description of the problem. We may need to inspect the device before confirming the quote, parts and timing.

Do you deliver?+

Free delivery Monday–Saturday where applicable within the normal Victoria Falls service area. Sunday delivery is available at an additional fee. Confirm the exact Sunday delivery charge on WhatsApp before delivery.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We currently accept Cash and EcoCash. Confirm your payment arrangement with us before purchasing.

How do I know whether a device is available?+

Listings show In Stock, Low Stock, Sold or Coming Soon. Sold devices cannot be ordered through their listing. Confirm current availability on WhatsApp before completing a transaction.
