Information you provide

Information you voluntarily provide may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email
  • Product or repair enquiry
  • Delivery information
  • Communications with the business

How information is used

The website primarily directs orders and enquiries through WhatsApp. Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Confirm availability
  • Arrange purchases
  • Provide repairs
  • Arrange collection or delivery
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain necessary business records

Lists stored in your browser

Saved products, comparison selections, enquiry lists and recently viewed products are stored as product identifiers in this browser. They contain no personal information, require no account and are not synchronised to another device. Use each list’s clear control to remove its stored selections. If browser storage is unavailable, lists work temporarily for the current visit. Repair and trade-in form details are not saved by this website.

Third-party services

Services such as WhatsApp have their own privacy practices and terms. Following an enquiry link opens WhatsApp; you choose whether to send the prepared message.

This website also loads some images from Unsplash. Requests to external image services disclose connection information such as your IP address to those services.

Communications and privacy questions

Internet communications cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure. Only share information needed to handle your enquiry.
